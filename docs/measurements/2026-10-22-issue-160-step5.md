## Triage (spec §7)

### Method

The method is root cause first: decide each uncertain call once, then each item from the calls
it reaches.

- **The calls an item reaches** are the four-kind edges (both halves, from the edge probe) of
  its root and of the root's `unknown_targets`.
  - For a span, the root set is the span's routines whose inherited status is not `complete`
    (spec §7's span definition).
  - For a fanout event, it is the event's subscribers.
  - For d45, it is the direct subscribers of the publisher's events.
- Every item reaches at least one edge.
- **Per-call verdicts** (spec §7):
  - `ambiguous-overload`: false.
  - `external-target`: true.
  - `member-not-found`: 0 on CDO.
  - `interface-open-world`: decided by the declared `Access` of the interface. I read all 57
    interface declarations that have such an edge, in the workspace source and in the
    sources inside the dependency `.app` packages: 23 are `Internal` (false), 34 are
    `Public` (true).
- **An item is epistemically false only if every call it reaches is false.**
- **Hazard.** Before the fold, every added d35 subscriber had `mayCommit = No`. So the engine's
  walk of the known closure, including the known implementations of every interface, found no
  Commit. On top of that, a source check: none of the implementations in the closure of the
  14 Public interfaces these items reach contains a `Commit(` call. The d35 hazard therefore
  exists only outside the closure: a third-party implementation of a Public interface, or the
  one external target. For d9, spans, fanout and d45, the hazard is "really reaches a call the
  analysis cannot finish", which holds for every epistemically true item.
- **Single-app** is sampled: 30 of the 71 added d35 and d9 findings, seed 160
  (`random.Random(160).shuffle`). All 71 were also run.

## Discoveries (precision issues, recorded in the issue ledger for filing)

- **D1: an `Internal` interface is emitted as `interface-open-world`.**
  - Scale: 23 interfaces, 109 cross-app edges and 9 single-app edges. Examples:
    `CSC ISubscription Cache` (Continia Core), `CDO Module License` (workspace),
    `IC Data Exchange` (Base Application) and 20 `CTS-CDN` interfaces.
  - Why it is wrong: an internal interface can only be implemented in its own app (and its
    friends), so the call is decidable from the implementations in the closure.
  - Effect on CDO: it alone accounts for 1 epistemically false d35 finding.
- **D2: an `ambiguous-overload` the AL compiler decides.** The repro is
  `this.IsSalesDocument(eDocCreationFactory.GetBusinessDocumentHeader().Number)` in
  `CTS-CDN eDoc. Creation Util` (Codeunit 6225538, Continia Delivery Network,
  `src/Al/eDocumentCreation/eDocCreationUtil.Codeunit.al:26`). There are 157 cross-app and 21
  single-app ambiguous-overload edges in all.
- **D3: an `external-target` on a same-app call whose callee has a body.**
  - The call: `CSC Onprem Licn. Mgt.GetDatabaseReader` (Codeunit 6192854) ->
    `CSC DB Reader.TryGetDatabaseReader` (Codeunit 6192855).
  - Both are in Continia Core, in its symbols and its shipped source.
  - The cross-app universe loads no body for the callee.
