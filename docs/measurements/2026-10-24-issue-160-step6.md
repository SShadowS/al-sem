## How the head was reached

1. **Task 1 stop rule (original design) failed.** `d` effects step E′ took 147.9 s against a bound
   of 200 ms; `o` took 2,131.8 s against 8.6 s. The cause was the per-fact witness search. The
   cone graph gives about 116 times more effect facts per root, and each one ran its own search.
   The claim stopped. The operator chose to split the work: digest only, ordering stays on the
   cut graph.
2. **Prototype A (boundary witnesses) failed.** Paths were cut at the first dependency routine.
   `d` E′ went from 7.1 s to 28.3-48.5 s and the peak from 4.77 GB to about 29 GB. The 88-root file
   was killed for memory at 32 GB in all 3 rounds.
3. **Prototype B (grouped boundary effects, "rule B") passed.** `d`: 1,020 effects (56 groups),
   E′ 3.06-3.16 s, peak 3.10-3.12 GB. `h`: 22,232 effects (862 groups), E′ 21.2-22.0 s, peak
   3.10-3.12 GB. d47/d49/d51 SARIF was byte-identical. The operator chose B, with fallback D
   stated in advance.
4. **Task 1′ re-measure on the real design (3 rounds)** passed all three stop rules (table below).
5. **Operator decisions.**
   - CentralGauge's bench held the CPU while Task 1′ ran, so its time bounds S1′ and S3′ were
     read on process CPU time. The wall-time bounds B1′ and B6 were left to the final bench.
   - The grading change (below) is accepted, with a follow-up filed.

## Determinism fix

- Commit `5406d96c` (attempt 8). Two anchorless `WordTemplates.Merge` rows in `d` (cross-app) swapped between
  runs, because unresolved-cone items were ordered by hash order. They now have a total order
  (column, then candidates, then call-site id). Final panel F1-O3 moved the column after the
  callee, so it only breaks the old key's ties.
- After the fix, two runs are identical. The new test failed when each tie-break was removed in
  turn, and passed after each revert.
- Master v2.0.3 was not affected: three runs of `d` are identical there, because its cone has no
  such rows.
