**An open observation, not a bound:** the head digest wall median is 7.9 s above the
parent's, inside the parent band (10.4 s), but the head was slower in 5 of the 6
interleaved pairs (the 6th a tie: 76.01 vs 76.01 s), also when it ran first in a round.
Ranking every span by head − parent median puts the whole difference in
`context.capability_cones` (+6.5 s; it contains `crossapp.context_build`'s +3.3 s);
every span outside the cones moved less than 0.5 s. Step 1b changes no cone code and no
cone input (the SARIF, the histogram and every census bucket outside `inputs` /
`program.snapshot_texts` are byte-identical), and analyze's cone span, also +4.7 s, sits
inside that command's noise. The cone walk is the most parallel, memory-bound part of
the run and has had the widest bands at every step (step 1: a +5.7 s head digest excess
over 3 rounds that reversed over the next 3). It is recorded so that step 2's A2.3
digest-saving bound, which reads against step 2's own parent (this head), is read with
it in mind; if step 2's parent digest wall stays near 80 s, the cone span is the place
to look.
