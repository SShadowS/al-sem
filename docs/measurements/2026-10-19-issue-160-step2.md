**`digest --single-app`, +11 MB post-attach.** This is the overlap the spec predicts (§4.4.4, §6.3). Single-app, the `l4` cell (bundle + SCC) is now alive during snapshot composition. At the parent, the SCC came into being after composition. It stays inside the 3 % band (bound 561 MB).

**prove, −1.66 GB.** The traces put this on A2.6's one-root change, not on the deleted context:

- At the parent, the peak (2250 MB) is set inside `digest.roots`. That is `compute_digest_effects_cli(.., None)`, the effects pass over EVERY root, 1 691 ms.
- At the head, prove passes `Some(from_ref(root))`, and `digest.roots` takes 0.1 ms.
- The deleted context (`prove.tx_context_map`) accounts for 331 ms of the 2.69 s wall saving.
- The prove output is byte-identical (A2.6).

**analyze, −99 MB post-attach.** The census below explains this:

- the context no longer builds its own cross-app graph (26.3 MB); it shares the base's;
- the base no longer holds a second copy of its injected edges (16.2 MB).

**query, −38 MB post-attach.** The Task 27 review found that the first version of Task 27 kept the whole cross-app base alive through the query. `View::into_query_parts` frees it again, as at the parent. The −38 MB is that fix plus the dropped edge copy.
