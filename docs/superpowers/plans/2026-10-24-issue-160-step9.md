**Goal:** `alsem prove`, `alsem fingerprint --routine …` and `alsem fingerprint --inventory-only`
build the cross-app `View` by default and read a tree composed over `rel.cone()`; `--single-app`
restores the base's bytes (apart from the stated `mayCommit` fix). Fingerprint B0, the other query forms and
`diff` are unchanged (OD-1 = S′).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-issue-160-step9-cross-app-prove-fingerprint-design.md`
("spec §n" below; revised after spec panel round `9 r1`). Test ids T1-T27 and fallbacks F1-F14 are the spec's.

**Architecture** (spec §4):
- `AnalysisTarget::build_with` and `build_cross_app_base_with` carry `--no-roots-config` (D1).
- `Plan.tree: TreeRead { None, Effects, FingerprintQuery, Inventory }`: every tree is read and dropped inside
  `prepare`, before `det` (D5). A new `Cell::TreeRead` holds the result; `View::take_tree_read`.
- prove = digest's pipeline with one root; `UnresolvedCone.gaps_in_cone`; boundary groups keep their full
  resource set for matching (D2).
- the `--routine` query reads a tree over `Roots(pool)`, with rule B in its witnesses, two role filters and
  `mayCommit` over the requested coverage (D3).
- inventory-only reads a tree over `Roots(∅)` through `assemble_full_snapshot`, the assembly half of
  `compose_full_snapshot` (D4).
- the gap derivation names `symbol-only-boundary` only for source-less apps (D9).

**Tech stack:** Rust. `scripts/agentflow` (`run`, `check-diff`), `scripts/ci-steps`,
`scripts/check-goldens`, `scripts/cdo-gate`, `scripts/bench_ab.py`, `/work/bench/160-10-11a/maxrss.py`,
the `discrimination-proof` and `cdo-measure` skills, the `golden-diff-triager` agent.
